# Software Supply Chain Breaches

Software supply-chain security is about knowing what enters a build, who can change it, and how to prove what was released.

## Defensive workflow

- Pin and review dependency changes.
- Generate software bills of materials where appropriate.
- Protect package publishing and CI credentials.
- Require signed commits, provenance, or trusted build attestations.
- Scan dependencies and containers before release.
- Maintain a fast process for revoking compromised artifacts.
